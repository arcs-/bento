Resizable panel layouts, written as HTML

Three custom elements for layouts you drag, collapse to a rail and turn into drawers on small screens. Any framework or none, under 10 kB.

Drag a divider, or tab to one and press Enter. This page is a bento box too; the lunch box is styled with a little CSS.

What it promises

  • Any framework. Plain HTML, React, Vue: no adapters.
  • Right first paint. Server-rendered panels never jump.
  • Animated collapse. Drags and window resizes stay instant.
  • Reduced motion. The size jumps, the content cross-fades.
  • Small screens. A modal media query makes a panel a drawer.
  • Accessible. The window splitter pattern, keys included.
  • Strict CSP. No inline styles, no exceptions.
