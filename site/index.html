Resizable panel layouts, written as HTML

bento is three custom elements, <bento-group>, <bento-panel> and <bento-separator>. Panels can be dragged, collapsed to nothing or to a rail, and become drawers on small screens. They work in any framework or none, lay out before the first paint, and animate only when a panel collapses.

Drag a divider in the lunch below. Tab to one and press Enter to collapse its compartment, or use the arrow keys. Double-click a divider to put its compartment back.

What it promises

Framework independent
Custom elements work in plain HTML, React 19, Vue and anything else that renders the DOM. There is no adapter to install, only JSX typings.
First paint is right
Load one small script blocking in the head, and server-rendered panels lay out as they are parsed. Nothing jumps when your app hydrates. Why it blocks
Collapsing animates
Collapsing and snapping animate; dragging, mounting and window resizes never do. Content keeps its size while a panel collapses, so nothing reflows per frame. How
Reduced motion
With prefers-reduced-motion, the size changes at once and the content cross-fades, so you still see what happened. Why a fade
Small screens
A panel with a modal media query becomes a drawer over the page when it matches, with Escape, back gesture and tap outside built in. Modal panels
Accessible
Separators follow the WAI-ARIA window splitter pattern: focusable, labelled by you, operated by arrow keys, Enter, Home and End. Accessibility

This page is a bento box too

The navigation, this text and the inspector are panels in one <bento-group>. Drag the dividers between them, collapse the navigation to its rail with Enter, or make the window narrow: below 1100 pixels the inspector becomes a drawer, below 720 the navigation does too, and the buttons in the title bar open them.
