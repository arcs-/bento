Resizable panel layouts, written as HTML

Three custom elements for layouts you can drag, collapse to a rail, and turn into drawers on small screens. In any framework or none, under 10 kB.

Drag a divider, or tab to one and press Enter. This lunch box is styled with a little CSS; the Styling guide shows it, and every other demo shows the default look.

What it promises

  • Any framework. Plain HTML, React, Vue: no adapters.
  • Right first paint. One blocking script; server-rendered panels never jump. Why
  • Animated collapse. Toggles animate; drags and window resizes don't.
  • Reduced motion. The size jumps and the content cross-fades. Why
  • Small screens. A modal media query turns a panel into a drawer. See it
  • Accessible. The WAI-ARIA window splitter pattern, keys included. Details
  • Strict CSP. No inline styles, no exceptions. How
