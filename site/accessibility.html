Accessibility

Separators follow the WAI-ARIA window splitter pattern. The names are yours.

What you write

…
  • A label on every separator, and on a modal panel, where it names the drawer.
  • aria-controls for browsers without ARIA element reflection.
  • tabindex="0" on separators a framework hydrates; elsewhere bento adds it.

Keys

On a focused separator Does
Arrows along the group 10px, 100px with Shift; below min they snap
Enter Toggle a collapsible primary panel
Home, End Primary panel to its min, max
Double-click Back to the starting size and state

Keys with Alt, Ctrl or Meta go to the browser. F6 is left to your app.

What bento does

  • Role, orientation and aria-valuenow, the primary panel's share from 0 to 100, through ElementInternals. Why
  • A panel collapsed to 0 hides its content with display: none. If focus was inside, it moves to the separator.
  • A modal panel is a real modal <dialog>: top layer, inert page, Escape to close.
  • Reduced motion: the size jumps and the content cross-fades. Why
  • System colors, so forced colors work; a 24px hit area, 40px on touch screens.
