Accessibility

Separators follow the WAI-ARIA window splitter pattern

What you need to write

…
  • A label on every separator and every modal panel, which names its drawer.
  • aria-controls, for browsers without ARIA element reflection.
  • tabindex="0" on separators a framework hydrates; elsewhere bento adds it.

Keys

On a focused separator Does
Arrows along the group 10px, 100px with Shift; below min they snap
Enter Toggle a collapsible primary panel
Home, End Primary panel to its min, max
Double-click Back to the starting size and state

Alt, Ctrl and Meta combinations go to the browser, F6 to your app.

What bento does

  • Role, orientation and aria-valuenow, the primary panel's share from 0 to 100, through ElementInternals.
  • A panel collapsed to 0 hides its content; focus inside moves to its separator.
  • A drawer is a modal <dialog>: top layer, inert page, Escape closes it.
  • Reduced motion: the size jumps and the content cross-fades.
  • A 24px hit area on the separators, 40px on touch screens.
