Live demos in bento's default look, each above the markup that runs it. Click a panel: the inspector compares its live size and collapsed with the starting state and logs its events.

Groups

A panel with a size keeps it between min and max, in px or %; panels without share the rest. Horizontal groups follow the writing direction, so right to left works.

Flexibleno size

A separator resizes its primary panel: the neighbour with a size or collapsible, the later one on a tie, or the one its aria-controls names.

Collapsing

A collapsible panel holds at min, then snaps to its collapsed-size past halfway: 0, or a rail that keeps its content. Enter on the separator toggles it, and so does writing collapsed.

Modal panels

While its modal query matches, a panel is a drawer that arrives collapsed: collapsed = false opens it, and Escape, back or a tap outside close it. Open the filters on the phone, then switch to desktop:

Events

Panels fire resize, resizeend, beforetoggle and toggle; none bubbles, so a parent listens with capture: true. Cancel beforetoggle to keep unsaved work: type in the notes, then collapse them.
